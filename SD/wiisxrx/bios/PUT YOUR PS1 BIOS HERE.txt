WiiStation needs a real PlayStation BIOS image in this folder. It is Sony
copyright, so it cannot ship with this channel -- dump it from your own console.

WiiStation probes for these filenames:

    SCPH1000.BIN  SCPH1001.BIN  SCPH1002.BIN
    SCPH5500.BIN  SCPH5501.BIN  SCPH5502.BIN

With this folder empty the channel still boots and still autoboots, and then
dies loading the ROM with "Invalid read from 0x1fc80000". HLE is offered in
WiiStation's menu but several paths expect a genuine image.

You can delete this file once the BIOS is in place.
